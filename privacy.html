Privacy Policy

Investment Portfolio is a private application used by its owner for personal investment research and automation.

Google user data

The application may access Google Drive data only after authorization by the account owner. Access is limited to the data and files needed for personal investment analysis, model-state storage, report generation, backups, and related automation.

Use of data

Google user data is used solely to provide the application's private functionality. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Storage and security

Application credentials are stored in protected service configuration such as encrypted repository secrets. OAuth tokens and Google user data are not intentionally published in this website or public source code.

Revoking access

The account owner may revoke the application's Google access at any time through Google Account security and connected-app settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the support email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
