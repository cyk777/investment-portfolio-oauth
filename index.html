Investment Portfolio

Investment Portfolio is a private investment research and portfolio automation tool used by its owner for personal investment analysis, model-state storage, report generation, and related workflows.

The application may request access to the owner's Google Drive in order to read and write files required by these private workflows. Access is used only for the owner's own data and automation.

This application is not offered as a public investment service and does not sell or share Google user data for advertising or marketing purposes.
